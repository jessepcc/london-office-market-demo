# Analyze submarkets

## Question and evidence

Which London areas differ under comparable evidence, and which remain unknown? Query requested geographies separately: City, West End, Canary Wharf, Midtown and Fringe, and Wider London. Retain Central London aggregates separately. Record each source's native boundaries, transparent mapped geography, unit, definition, category and reference period. A publisher's Docklands or Fringe boundary may not match another's; do not relabel it by intuition.

## Analysis, example and exhibit

Test whether a reported divergence survives matched measure, period and quality mix. Use compare with submarkets only on compatible pairs and explain publisher coverage limits. Do not sum overlapping areas or mix Central London aggregates into their components. Wider London is an evidence requirement, not a residual invented by subtracting Central London from another total. A missing area is an honest coverage gap, not zero demand or unchanged rents.

A named Canary Wharf lease can illustrate a local occupier move, but it cannot resolve the area's market trend or stand for all London. Same-period compatible submarket bars or histories can show supported differences; incompatible boundaries require source-labelled tables or text. Material landlord interpretation concerns relative competition and occupier choices within stated catchments. Look for contrary local deals, sector mix and quality effects before attributing all variation to location. Finish with areas actually covered, retained dates, incompatible boundaries and explicit Wider London gaps; do not title a Central London-only result as a complete whole-London assessment.

## Complete the analysis

Query relevant evidence first, including qualitative categories without a metric filter. Inspect totals, next_offset, supporting excerpts and conflicts before inferring absence. Search and retrieve when the evidence is missing or too old for the question. Cache exact excerpts as candidate evidence. This package cannot approve or normalize monitoring feeds.

Test the proposed mechanism against the strongest available counterevidence and identify a dated catalyst or observable follow-up that would change the interpretation. Finish with scoped cited facts, labeled interpretation, publisher forecasts, monitorable watchpoints and material gaps. Check each excerpt against its claim's dates, geography, definition and strength. Unknown dates remain unknown. State the evidence boundary and retained old periods relative to the run date.

Use compare for compatible arithmetic. For a useful exhibit explicitly load choose_visualization, then prepare_visualization_data, and use the prepared chart ID. Missing history warrants a table or text. Follow the shared run budget and finish before exhaustion.

When preparing a full-market report, reference one actual supported finding or explicit gap for every area in the harness catalog. Central London and London-wide macro are not evidence for named local areas. City Fringe leaves Midtown unmeasured, wider districts are selective, and one Canary Wharf commitment leaves local market-wide metrics unknown. Keep these gaps in the saved coverage references. Inspect the inventory's unpaginated local candidates for compatible same-period comparisons.

Source views use a source-bound intent: {"kind":"interpretation","interpretation_role":"source_view","source_view":{"evidence_id":"registered commentary ID"}}. Omit overrides; measurements or unregistered commentary cannot support it. Agent interpretations use the two source-bound analysis intents in research_market.
