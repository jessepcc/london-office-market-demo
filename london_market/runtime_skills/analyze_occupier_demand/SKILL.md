# Analyze occupier demand

## Question and evidence

Which occupier preferences are demonstrated by behavior, and which are stated intentions? Separate signed leasing, active requirements, surveys, attendance, utilization and space-per-worker measures. Distinguish certification, measured building performance, occupier ESG preference and legal requirements. Record survey sponsor, sample, response rate when known, geography, fieldwork date, sector composition and self-selection; a broker-client sample is not the London workforce.

## Analysis, example and exhibit

Test whether quality/ESG preference persists beyond high-rent trophy deals and whether hybrid work changes space use, size or configuration. Seek lower-cost moves, renewals and contrary attendance evidence. One certified building with a strong rent cannot establish an ESG premium; location, age, quality and incentives confound the comparison. No current tool supports causal adjustment or regression. Legal claims require jurisdiction, applicability, effective date and current authoritative status; a consultation is not an enacted duty.

A named occupier's signed move to a certified building illustrates one choice when date, area and certification are supported. Separate the occupier's stated reason from analyst interpretation; do not infer it from a marketing label. Use compatible observed demand series for charts, while survey/attendance claims often need labelled text or a methods table. Do not chart anecdotes as market share. Landlord implications concern evidenced specification preferences and uncertainty over fit-out/flexibility, without prescribing capex or asset value. Missing effective rents, matched comparators, survey methodology or behavior-versus-intent evidence must remain explicit gaps.

## Complete the analysis

Search and retrieve when the evidence is missing or too old for the question. Cache exact excerpts as candidate evidence. This package cannot approve or normalize monitoring feeds.

Test the proposed mechanism against the strongest available counterevidence and identify a dated catalyst or observable follow-up that would change the interpretation. Finish with scoped cited facts, labeled interpretation, publisher forecasts, monitorable watchpoints and material gaps. Check each excerpt against its claim's dates, geography, definition and strength. Unknown dates remain unknown. State the evidence boundary and retained old periods relative to the run date.

Use compare for compatible arithmetic. For a useful exhibit explicitly load choose_visualization, then prepare_visualization_data, and use the prepared chart ID. Missing history warrants a table or text. Follow the shared run budget and finish before exhaustion.
