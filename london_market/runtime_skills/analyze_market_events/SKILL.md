# Analyze market events

## Question and evidence

What actually changed, when did it happen, and why could it matter to London office leasing? Record actors, building/location, event date, announcement date, retrieval date, scale if disclosed, source origin and current status. Distinguish rumor, proposal, planning approval, contractual commitment, completion, cancellation and correction. Prefer primary company, planning, transport or policy statements, then independent reporting with a named evidential basis.

## Analysis, example and exhibit

Test materiality through area, occupier significance, timing or credible policy impact, rather than headline count. A newly reported historical lease remains a historical event; do not label its discovery a new letting. Repeated syndicated stories are one event and one originating claim, not independent confirmation. Seek subsequent corrections or changed status. Policy announcements need effective dates and applicability; proposed transport improvements do not prove a present rent uplift.

A named occupier's dated completed lease with building, area and exact source excerpt is a useful example, with its limits. A proposed development merits a conditional delivery watchpoint. Chronological narrative or a source-labelled status table often serves better than a numeric chart; news volume is not a measure of leasing momentum. Use charts only for compatible normalized measurements through approved exhibit tools. Explain landlord relevance as interpretation and follow-up conditions, not predicted asset appreciation. Expose missing event dates, unconfirmed terms, uncertain statuses, duplicate coverage and unsupported causal mechanisms. Prioritize material verified changes over a list of every article.

## Complete the analysis

Query relevant evidence first, including qualitative categories without a metric filter. Inspect totals, next_offset, supporting excerpts and conflicts before inferring absence. Search and retrieve when the evidence is missing or too old for the question. Cache exact excerpts as candidate evidence. This package cannot approve or normalize monitoring feeds.

Test the proposed mechanism against the strongest available counterevidence and identify a dated catalyst or observable follow-up that would change the interpretation. Finish with scoped cited facts, labeled interpretation, publisher forecasts, monitorable watchpoints and material gaps. Check each excerpt against its claim's dates, geography, definition and strength. Unknown dates remain unknown. State the evidence boundary and retained old periods relative to the run date.

Use compare for compatible arithmetic. For a useful exhibit explicitly load choose_visualization, then prepare_visualization_data, and use the prepared chart ID. Missing history warrants a table or text. Follow the shared run budget and finish before exhaustion.

Material news coverage references the exact announcement/status finding. One local announcement can support a local qualitative area row with its missing market-wide measurements; it cannot imply a complete submarket series.

Source views use a source-bound intent: {"kind":"interpretation","interpretation_role":"source_view","source_view":{"evidence_id":"registered commentary ID"}}. Omit overrides; measurements or unregistered commentary cannot support it. Agent interpretations use the two source-bound analysis intents in research_market.
