# Analyze supply pipeline

## Question and evidence

What competing office space could arrive in the relevant location and period, and how certain is its delivery? Record project/building identity, address, area basis, source date, proposed versus actual completion, status, new construction versus refurbishment, prelet status and committed versus speculative space. Separate proposed, consented, under construction and completed phases. Marketing availability and pipeline are different concepts. A completed occupied prelet is not speculative competition.

## Analysis, example and exhibit

Test the proposed shortage against plausible delivery, slippage and refurbishment alternatives. Prefer developer/project disclosures, planning decisions and dated publisher pipeline methods; planning consent does not prove funding, commencement or delivery. Deduplicate project aliases and overlapping phases conceptually, but do not calculate a project total: current tools have no approved pipeline aggregation. A quoted publisher total can be cited as that publisher's estimate with scope and definitions; do not add it to individual projects.

A named refurbishment example needs dated construction status, supported area basis, expected completion and prelet fraction if disclosed. Explain timing risk and competing catchment without assuming all area will be available. Use source-labelled status/timing descriptions; a validated publisher under-construction series may support a chart, while project-only evidence warrants text and an explicit chart/data gap. Watchpoints include nearby delivery, quality differentiation and uncommitted space. Missing project status, unknown phases or undisclosed prelets prevent a precise speculative-supply conclusion. A consent headline is not evidence of near-term supply relief.

## Complete the analysis

Query relevant evidence first, including qualitative categories without a metric filter. Inspect totals, next_offset, supporting excerpts and conflicts before inferring absence. Search and retrieve when the evidence is missing or too old for the question. Cache exact excerpts as candidate evidence. This package cannot approve or normalize monitoring feeds.

Test the proposed mechanism against the strongest available counterevidence and identify a dated catalyst or observable follow-up that would change the interpretation. Finish with scoped cited facts, labeled interpretation, publisher forecasts, monitorable watchpoints and material gaps. Check each excerpt against its claim's dates, geography, definition and strength. Unknown dates remain unknown. State the evidence boundary and retained old periods relative to the run date.

Use compare for compatible arithmetic. For a useful exhibit explicitly load choose_visualization, then prepare_visualization_data, and use the prepared chart ID. Missing history warrants a table or text. Follow the shared run budget and finish before exhaustion.

Account separately for new development, refurbishment and prelets in full-market coverage. Combined completion totals do not establish separate new-build or refurbishment measurements. Reviewed starts/individual-building passages can support correctly qualified qualitative findings. A named pre-let remains a specific transaction unless a normalized source definition and period support an aggregate. Use distinct referenced claims or explicit gaps, never a single supply tag as proof of all three.

Source views use a source-bound intent: {"kind":"interpretation","interpretation_role":"source_view","source_view":{"evidence_id":"registered commentary ID"}}. Omit overrides; measurements or unregistered commentary cannot support it. Agent interpretations use the two source-bound analysis intents in research_market.
