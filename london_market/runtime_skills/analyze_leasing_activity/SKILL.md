# Analyze leasing activity

## Question and evidence

Is leasing momentum strengthening, and is available competition actually tightening? Vacancy is unoccupied stock at a date; availability is marketed space and can include occupied space offered for later letting. Preserve each publisher's denominator and boundary. Take-up is leased area over a period under a stated transaction definition. Net absorption is change in occupied stock; gross take-up does not establish absorption. Active requirements and under-offer space indicate intent or negotiation, not completed leasing.

## Analysis, example and exhibit

Test whether higher take-up coincides with reduced compatible vacancy, while considering completions, releases and large one-off deals. Obtain quarter versus year-to-date periods, area units, stock basis, exclusions, renewals and prelet treatment. Do not annualize a partial quarter or combine quarterly and cumulative flows. Use compare only for compatible pairs; vacancy versus availability must return a refusal, not a spread presented as change. A rising flow and rising vacancy can coexist when supply or releases increase.

A named occupier's completed lease with event date, building, area and whether a relocation/prelet/renewal illustrates transaction mix. Under-offer space is a watchpoint with its status, not achieved take-up. Use separate compatible vacancy histories and take-up bars; show stock and flow in distinct exhibits. Material implications concern competing marketed space, letting velocity and negotiation uncertainty. Expose absent absorption, denominators, incomplete quarters or requirement conversion evidence. One large letting cannot establish broad demand recovery.

Test the proposed mechanism against the strongest available counterevidence and identify a dated catalyst or observable follow-up that would change the interpretation. Finish with scoped cited facts, labeled interpretation, publisher forecasts, monitorable watchpoints and material gaps. Check each excerpt against its claim's dates, geography, definition and strength. Unknown dates remain unknown. State the evidence boundary and retained old periods relative to the run date.

Use compare for compatible arithmetic. For a useful exhibit explicitly load choose_visualization, then prepare_visualization_data, and use the prepared chart ID. Missing history warrants a table or text. Follow the shared run budget and finish before exhaustion.

Prelets may enter take-up when signed, even if occupation is later. Do not exclude an announced commitment from present-year take-up solely because occupation is future. Require reported area, transaction date and publisher measurement rules before deriving a quantified take-up contribution.

Vacancy, availability and leasing are separate full-market coverage lanes. Source-reported supply with unconfirmed marketed-availability equivalence cannot satisfy an availability measure. Reference exact lane support or its gap, not a shared availability hashtag.
