# Analyze supported changes

Query relevant observations and inspect exact supporting excerpts, conflicts and reference periods. Use compare with exactly two observation IDs. Choose trend for dated endpoints or submarkets for same-period areas. Publisher, definition, unit, metric, category, geography and cadence must support the comparison. An incompatible result is a limitation, not a derived number.

Use the tool's absolute_change, relative_growth_percent, unit, baseline and endpoint periods and evidence IDs. A percentage measurement changes in percentage points. Relative growth is unavailable at a zero baseline. Preserve rounding. Ambiguous rent periods, conflicting values, missing or overlapping periods must not become a trend. Never invent aggregation, imputation, annualization or causal estimates.

Write a cited fact with measure, geography, endpoints and change. Interpret its landlord relevance separately. Two points cannot establish persistence or cause. Inspect contrary evidence or intervening periods when material and affordable. State retained old periods relative to the run date and unresolved mechanisms. Finish within the shared budget.

Source views use a source-bound intent: {"kind":"interpretation","interpretation_role":"source_view","source_view":{"evidence_id":"registered commentary ID"}}. Omit overrides; measurements or unregistered commentary cannot support it. Agent interpretations use the two source-bound analysis intents in research_market.
