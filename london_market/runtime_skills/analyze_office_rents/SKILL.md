# Analyze office rents

## Question and evidence

Is the observed rent movement broad repricing, a change in building quality, or a change in lease terms? Define prime and Grade A using the publisher's criteria; neither label is universal. Obtain source series with unit (such as GBP per square foot per year), area basis, quarter, geography, specification and rent basis. Separate asking rents, headline achieved rents and net effective rents. Record rent-free period, lease length, breaks, fit-out, service charges and other incentives when available. A headline rent without incentive detail cannot establish effective income.

## Analysis, example and exhibit

Compare only compatible measures through compare. A Grade A premium needs a genuinely comparable baseline and matched period/geography; a trophy lease versus an all-stock average is composition evidence, not a calculated quality premium. Do not manufacture net effective rent from incomplete terms. Hypothesis: tighter high-quality supply supports asking-rent growth. Counterevidence: generous incentives, vacant prime space or rents concentrated in a few exceptional deals.

A useful example is a named building's dated achieved letting with supported area, rent and lease basis, contrasted with the publisher's market series. Explain whether the deal merely illustrates the upper end. Compatible asking-rent histories can support a line; compatible same-period submarkets can support bars. Keep different rent bases in separate labelled tables. Watchpoints for landlords are positioning against comparable competition, incentive transparency and quality segmentation; do not infer asset value or prescribe a rent from market averages. If achieved rents, incentives or Grade A definitions are absent, explicitly report that gap rather than answer a premium question with asking rents.

## Complete the analysis

Query relevant evidence first, including qualitative categories without a metric filter. Inspect totals, next_offset, supporting excerpts and conflicts before inferring absence. Search and retrieve when the evidence is missing or too old for the question. Cache exact excerpts as candidate evidence. This package cannot approve or normalize monitoring feeds.

Test the proposed mechanism against the strongest available counterevidence and identify a dated catalyst or observable follow-up that would change the interpretation. Finish with scoped cited facts, labeled interpretation, publisher forecasts, monitorable watchpoints and material gaps. Check each excerpt against its claim's dates, geography, definition and strength. Unknown dates remain unknown. State the evidence boundary and retained old periods relative to the run date.

Use compare for compatible arithmetic. For a useful exhibit explicitly load choose_visualization, then prepare_visualization_data, and use the prepared chart ID. Missing history warrants a table or text. Follow the shared run budget and finish before exhaustion.

Prime rents and Grade A rents are separate required coverage lanes. Reference their inspected exact measurements/passages independently. Averaging windows must match for local comparisons; quarter, cumulative January-May and H1 averages are not one rent trend.
