# Prepare the London office briefing

Use the shared runner and pinned weekly context. Begin with query_evidence published_since=window_start, published_until=window_end, offset=0 and no other filters. Inspect its actual result, including empty results; completion requires this check. Page material truncated results. Inspect market_inventory for latest periods and gaps, then target current releases and missing local support through search/retrieve/cache_evidence. Weekly search uses the publication window; request older windows for necessary history. Follow protected completion reserves in Run budget; repairs are not guaranteed. Avoid skill reloads and repeated broad queries.

## Research and chronology

Cover rents; leasing, vacancy and availability; supply, refurbishment and prelets; submarkets; macro and employment; occupier quality, ESG and hybrid drivers; and news. Resolve stale or missing lanes with bounded research, releases or announcements. Use attributed secondary reporting when useful; syndication is not independent corroboration. Retained retrieval is not a current check.
Inspect dates and source methodology on the actual page. Keep publication date, event date, reference period, first ingestion and retrieval separate. A new publication about an old quarter is new publication evidence and historical measurement context. Late discovery is not a new event. Absence from the prior briefing means not previously cited, not previously unknown. An exact same-period changed value is a revision, not week-over-week movement. No prior report does not prevent a comparison using independently retrieved and reviewed same-series history.

New sources are candidate qualitative evidence until the reviewed monitoring process admits their definitions and measurements. Name the publisher in each candidate card. Search snippets cannot support figures. Read subsequent document slices or evidence pages when truncated. Newly retrieved commentary remains attributed interpretation unless the excerpt establishes an observed fact. An announcement or planning submission supports its stated planned status, not completed occupation, a signed lease or realized take-up.

Explicitly account for City, West End, Canary Wharf, Midtown and Fringe, and Wider London. Preserve source-native boundaries. City Fringe is not Midtown; selected wider-London districts are samples. Central London does not establish whole-London coverage. UK or Great Britain macro and hybrid data are contextual rather than London office measurements. No new evidence never establishes unchanged market conditions.

## Measurements and exhibits

Use compare for every calculated difference or growth rate. State the actual endpoints. Monthly and quarterly observations remain dated context. Only compare results explicitly marked week_over_week support that label. The harness requires reviewed source-defined adjacent seven-day totals or actual stock snapshots seven days apart. Two daily flow values seven days apart are not weekly totals. Report an explicit weekly indicator gap when no such measurement exists; do not interpolate quarterly figures, count news as a market index, or label a monthly change weekly.

Run budget.visual_purposes and market_inventory.visual_purposes use complete retained observations before pagination. Include supported trend, relationship, displayed-flow sum and proportion graphs. Inspect exact excerpts; choose missing candidates early in one batch, then prepare returned plan IDs in another. Select prepared IDs in finish.chart_ids. Omitted prepared charts and fallback tables cannot satisfy attainable purposes. The native-local exhibit remains required unless a suitable selected graph serves both. Choice/preparation actions, model turns and finish must preserve classification protection. Unsupported reasons become saved limitations. Preserve definitions, dates, independent axis units and geography. Relations are descriptive; shares invent no remainder. Chart claims cite selected chart_ids; narratives and forecasts cite original evidence_ids.

Keep vacancy distinct from availability, asking distinct from achieved rents, new construction distinct from refurbishment, and pipeline distinct from currently available space. Space acquired including owner-occupier purchases cannot be spliced into a leased-only take-up series. Achieved transaction-average rents may reflect deal mix. Different averaging windows cannot establish a rent trend. Certification premiums do not establish a widening gap or a causal amenity effect. Gross completions do not establish net additions. Starts counts do not establish future pipeline area or delivery timing. Unlet future construction can still compete, even though construction is not currently available space.

Quarter-only excerpts have no inferred event day. Lease expiry does not establish handback, released area or re-letting. A planned occupation date is not a signing date.

## Cards and completion

Every card needs one to three unique topic_tags from the canonical card topic_tags vocabulary supplied by the harness. Use only relevant tags. Do not emit a signal or a per-card reason. The harness asks Clef to assign bullish, bearish or neutral to each audited observed fact from a landlord leasing perspective. Provider failure leaves a fact unassessed and the report partial. An oversized state or unfit action plan rejects completion before Clef.

Landlord analysis uses only a source-bound intent in the same ordered claims list: {"kind":"interpretation","interpretation_role":"landlord_analysis","analysis":{"kind":"delivery_uncertainty","forecast_evidence_id":"exact reviewed forecast excerpt ID"}} or {"kind":"interpretation","interpretation_role":"landlord_analysis","analysis":{"kind":"lease_commitment","transaction_evidence_id":"exact reviewed completed pre-let excerpt ID"}}. The harness supplies the complete prose, headline, citations and tags; omit all overrides. Two distinct constructors with exact source identities satisfy the analysis minimum; aliases do not. Source views use a source-bound intent: {"kind":"interpretation","interpretation_role":"source_view","source_view":{"evidence_id":"registered commentary ID"}}. Omit overrides; measurements or unregistered commentary cannot support it. Other free kinds still need source review.

Finish typed claims, prepared chart IDs and coverage; omit live sections. Limitations are qualitative.

Pure audits preserve bounds, units, periods and referents in all headlines/cards. Inspect query_evidence.source_qualifications: forecast totals require the future-supply denominator, unstarted construction and delivery risk in the same card. Inspected discrepancies use the pair intent below. Hybrid coverage requires attendance/hybrid/RTO subjects, not footprint/talent. Independently review all source entailment, pricing/rankings/causality and landlord implications; guards do not certify semantics.

## Required report coverage

The harness supplies the canonical required coverage catalog and unpaginated retained_coverage_pointers. Finish.coverage uses only {"scope":"full_market","gaps":{"requirement_id":["qualitative evidence limitation"]}}. Do not allocate indexes, basis or chart attachments. The harness binds substantive selected claims and exhibits to exact eligible lanes and derives basis, dates and invariant qualifications. This is citation eligibility, not semantic proof: write separate narrowly cited substantive findings for the actual subjects; a watchpoint alone does not close a lane.

Inspect exact retained pointer IDs with query_evidence even when their periods predate this week. Use useful dated context or, after inspection, provide an explicit applicability/methodology exclusion in coverage.gaps. Do not erase context merely because it is old, or claim nothing was retrieved when retained support is available. Conduct bounded targeted research for actual material omissions. Current source updates remain distinct from historical measurements; neither creates a weekly comparison.

query_evidence.coverage_support identifies exact reviewed passage facets. Prime and Grade A qualitative passages retain unknown averaging periods. Employment is separate from rates and economy; quality and ESG from hybrid work; marketed availability from reported supply and vacancy; new construction from refurbishment. New candidates use the exact canonical leaf requirement_id as cache_evidence.category for qualified qualitative/context support. Broad macro or occupier categories and tags do not prove leaf support. Candidate category assignment is structural eligibility, not semantic entailment or monitoring approval.

Prepare native-local exhibits covering every attainable named area group in Run budget.required_local_exhibit or market_inventory.local_exhibit_candidates before finish. A City/West End pair alone cannot cover available Fringe/wider district evidence. Use exact observation IDs and preferred=submarkets through choose_visualization then prepare_visualization_data. Include the prepared chart ID in finish.chart_ids; coverage binding is automatic. Preserve useful aggregate context. Incompatible averaging periods require separate exhibits or an honest table, never a fabricated match.


Card tags are not coverage keys: leasing uses demand, economy uses macro; geographies belong in prose.

Separate statistics, estimates and forecasts; attribute GLA mechanisms as interpretation. For ONS May-July 2026 use separate clauses: employment 73.9% and inactivity 20.5% concern London residents aged 16–64; unemployment 6.8% concerns economically active London residents aged 16+. Its denominator is those in work plus those seeking and available to work. Cite the exact London row and same-document age header, unemployment glossary and residence-definition leaves. These are not London office-job rates. Label professional job vacancies or recruitment explicitly; keep them separate from office vacancy.

Reviewed H1 Grade A City/West End rents share H1 periods. Separate January-May/YTD prime rents and passages with unknown averaging windows. Scope absence claims to inspected sources.

Repair qualitative metadata as well as cards; statistics belong in cited claims. Check cited_kind_counts and required_cited_kind_minima before drops. Inspect related_evidence_ids or repair_evidence_ids for exact definitions and narrower source passages; source identity alone does not establish support.

Keep company/area scope, actual measurement windows and denominators. Known building area does not establish take-up or availability. Split estate levels from visitor forecasts. Declining aggregate vacancy and a flat overlapping submarket do not establish a stable overall supply-demand balance.

Prefer twenty to twenty-four substantive cards; forty is the ceiling. Account for every coverage lane using findings, selected exhibits or inspected gaps. Retain two distinct analyses and two cited watchpoints. Omit redundant endpoint cards covered by typed comparisons; preserve qualifications.

Reviewed publisher/endpoint discrepancies require {"kind":"fact","comparison":{"kind":"publisher_endpoint_discrepancy","from_observation_id":"executed baseline ID","to_observation_id":"executed endpoint ID"}} in finish.claims or indexed revision. Use an actual compare’s ordered pair; omit overrides. A source qualification with requires_comparison needs that comparison before asserting its published change. The harness authors prose, headline, full citations and tags, preserves both changes and labels rounded percentages. In reporting tasks this is a Clef-assessed fact, with no analysis credit. Endpoint-only and unrelated cards stay ordinary cited claims.
