# Landing page design

The page uses an editorial layout with a large serif heading and an actual chart screenshot in its first viewport. Pale green paper, dark green text and restrained teal connect the presentation to its evidence charts. Section density varies between the introduction, recordings, workflow, trial instructions and coverage limits.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| --paper | #f4f6f2 | Main reading background. |
| --ink | #193e3a | Text and live-demo section. |
| --muted | #526962 | Secondary text on paper. |
| --teal | #176e64 | Emphasis and focus on the evidence theme. |
| --line | #cbd4cb | Structural rules. |
| --mint | #dce9dc | Recorded exhibit frame. |

Georgia headings pair with system sans serif body text. The layout has a 1256px content limit and collapses to a single column below 760px. Videos use native controls, do not autoplay and preload no media. Only one recording plays at a time. Reduced-motion preferences disable the single exhibit entrance and smooth scrolling.

Keep the source and date captions next to recordings. Do not add decorative market metrics or imply the historical exhibit is live.
